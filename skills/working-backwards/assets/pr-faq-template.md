# Working backwards PR and FAQ template

This is an internal planning document written from a proposed future. Label assumptions and evidence gaps so future tense does not become a false launch claim.

```text
Idea or initiative: [Name]
Document owner: [Name or Owner needed]
Decision requested: [Decision]
Decision date: [Date or Date needed]
Proposed launch date: [Proposed date or Date needed]
Status: Internal planning draft

Five customer questions
1. Customer: [Specific customer or segment]
2. Problem or opportunity: [Supported problem or Evidence needed]
3. Most important benefit: [Supported or proposed benefit]
4. Evidence of need: [Research, feedback, behavior, or Evidence needed]
5. Proposed experience: [How the customer discovers, uses, and benefits]

Future press release
Headline: [Write last]
One-sentence summary: [Customer, proposed experience, and main benefit]
Future date: [Proposed date]

Summary
[Put the customer and most important benefit first. Explain the proposal in plain language.]

Customer problem or opportunity
[Describe without exaggeration. Separate evidence from assumptions.]

Proposed approach
[Explain the experience before naming features.]

Evidence
| Claim | Source | Status | Evidence owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [claim] | [source or Missing] | [supported, assumption, disputed] | [Name or Owner needed] | [Date or Date needed] |

Customer experience
[Discovery, setup, main task, support, and result.]

Proposed customer reaction, not a quote
[Paraphrase the hoped-for reaction and label it as a hypothesis.]

Call to action at launch
[What the customer could do if the proposal ships]

Customer FAQ
Q: Who is this for?
A: [Answer]

Q: What problem does it solve?
A: [Answer and evidence]

Q: How would I get started?
A: [Answer or Unknown]

Q: What limitations would I see?
A: [Answer or Unknown]

Q: How would I get help?
A: [Answer or Unknown]

Stakeholder FAQ
Q: What decision is needed now?
A: [Decision]

Q: Why now?
A: [Evidence or Assumption]

Q: What alternatives were considered?
A: [Options and tradeoffs]

Q: What is the smallest useful scope?
A: [Scope]

Q: What is explicitly out of scope?
A: [Exclusions]

Q: What must be true for this to work?
A: [Dependencies and assumptions]

Q: What are the main risks and mitigations?
A: [Risks, owners, and dates]

Q: How will success be judged?
A: [Supported signals, baselines, and decision rules]

Q: What would cause us to stop?
A: [Stop conditions]

Visual brief
Most important scenario: [Scenario]
Low-fidelity format: [Sketch, flow, storyboard, or experience map]
What the visual must make clear: [Experience or decision]
Open visual question: [Question]

Open questions and actions
- [Question or action]. Owner: [Name or Owner needed]. Due: [Date or Date needed].
```
