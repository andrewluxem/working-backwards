# Working backwards question bank

Select questions that test the specific idea. Do not attach the full bank as filler, and do not answer from assumption.

## Decision and vision

- What decision or guidance is needed now?
- What is the proposed customer experience?
- Which part of the idea remains a hypothesis?
- Why is this the right time to decide?

## Customer and problem

- Who is the specific customer?
- What are they trying to accomplish?
- What problem or opportunity does the evidence show?
- Which segments may experience the problem differently?
- What do customers do today instead?
- What would disappoint them about the proposed experience?

## Experience and support

- How would a customer discover and start using it?
- What is the main task from start to result?
- What access, permission, location, or device limits apply?
- What happens when the experience fails?
- How would a customer get help?

## Scope and alternatives

- What is the smallest useful scope?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- Which alternatives were considered?
- Why is the proposed approach better for the customer than each alternative?
- Which current process or product could solve the problem without new work?

## Feasibility and risk

- What must be true for the proposal to work?
- Which dependencies, capabilities, or approvals are unresolved?
- What are the main customer, operational, privacy, security, financial, and delivery risks?
- How would each risk be reduced, detected, or accepted?
- Which topics are disputed, and who decides them?

## Measurement and stop conditions

- Which customer behavior or outcome would show value?
- What baseline exists, and what is still unmeasured?
- Which decision rule will be used at review?
- When will the result be reviewed, and by whom?
- What evidence would cause the team to narrow, pause, or stop?

## Answer rules

- Put frequent and decision-critical questions first.
- Answer customer FAQs in customer language.
- Separate supported facts from assumptions.
- Use `Unknown` or `Evidence needed` when the source cannot answer.
- Give open questions an owner and date.
